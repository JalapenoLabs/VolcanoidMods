# Changelog

2026.11.23 - Initial release. Once an hour clouds roll in and it rains for ten minutes: light rain, heavy rain, or a thunderstorm whose lightning can strike players and cogs. Recorded rain, loud outside and muffled under a roof, puddles, wet hulls, rough seas, steaming lava, and gusts of wind that strip leaves from the trees and stream smoke downwind. A Weather volume slider in the audio options. Client-side only: players with the mod share the same storms, players without it are unaffected.
