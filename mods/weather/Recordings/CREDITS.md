# Recordings

The rain the mod plays is recorded, not synthesised. Both recordings are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); no credit is required, and it is given gladly.

| File | Recording | Author | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ylmir-rain-heavy.ogg` | "Rain (loopable)", loop 2 | Ylmir | [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable) |
| `ylmir-rain-light.ogg` | "Rain (loopable)", loop 3 | Ylmir | [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable) |

`render.py` turns them into the loops the mod ships in `Content/Sounds/`: the open-air rain, and the same rain as
heard through a drillship's hull. Thunder and wind are still synthesised at load (`Source/Synth/`).
