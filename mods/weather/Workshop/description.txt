The island gets weather. Rain & Thunderstorms brings [b]rain and lightning storms[/b] to Volcanoids.
About once an hour, clouds roll in over a few minutes and darken, and it rains for ten minutes before the sky slowly clears again. Each storm is one of three kinds:
[list]
[*][b]Light rain:[/b] grey clouds that still let some sky through, a light shower and small puddles.
[*][b]Heavy rain:[/b] dark clouds, dim light, a downpour with mist on the horizon, and puddles that spread.
[*][b]Thunderstorm:[/b] heavy rain with lightning. Bolts crawl through the clouds and light them up from inside, and thunder rolls in after them, sooner and louder the closer they strike.
[/list]
Lightning lands mostly at random, but it prefers tall things: a strike close to a player, a COG or a drillship jumps to it. A direct hit hurts, so head for cover when the thunder gets close.
[h2]What comes with the storm[/h2]
[list]
[*]Rain loud and clear outside, muffled and cozy inside a drillship, a mine or a shelter, and drumming on the hull when you stand next to a drillship.
[*]Raindrops landing on your view and running down it while you're out in the open.
[*]Puddles that gather on open ground, ripple in the rain and dry up afterwards.
[*]Glossy wet ground and hulls, rain splashing off the drillships and pouring off their roof edges.
[*]Wind you can see: gusts tear leaves from the trees and blow them downwind, drive sheets of spray across the ground, ripple the grass and stream the drillships' chimney smoke off sideways.
[*]A rising sea: bigger swells, whitecaps and chop.
[*]Lava pools that steam all the time and boil off great white clouds in the rain, thick enough that you can barely see through them.
[/list]
Underground, all of it stops, and it comes back as you reach the surface.
[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
[b]Client-side only.[/b] Install it whether you host, join or play alone. It only changes what you see and hear: nothing is sent over the network, it never blocks joining a game, and friends without it are unaffected.
Friends who also run the mod get [b]the same storms at the same moment, down to every lightning bolt[/b], even if the host doesn't have it, because every copy reads the weather from the same clock.
Lightning only does damage when the host has the mod (the game applies damage on the host). It hurts the host's character and COGs; friends who join see the same bolts without taking damage.
[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Works with [b]Day/Night Cycle[/b]: storms come by day and by night, and storm clouds hide the moon and stars.
[*]Loading a save made without the mod shows the game's mod-mismatch dialog: choose [b]Continue[/b] to play it with the weather on.
[*]The weather runs on real time: storms keep coming while the game is paused, and a save does not remember its weather.
[*]Armour soaks up a strike like any other hit, and being under a roof keeps you safe from lightning entirely.
[*]A [b]Weather[/b] volume slider in the game's audio options; the storm schedule itself is fixed.
[/list]
